Overview
Littlebird is an "always-on" AI personal assistant that runs at the OS level on Mac (with companion iOS/Android apps for chat and recall on the go), continuously reading on-screen content across all applications — documents, meetings, emails, browser tabs — without requiring the user to manually feed it context, and without storing screenshots. Rather than a chatbot prompted from a blank slate, it builds a persistent, real-time understanding of a user's workday so that when asked a question ("what did I do today?"), it already has the context. Features include a Daily Journal (now available in 12 languages), Meeting Notes/transcription, "Routines" for recurring automated prompts, and privacy controls to exclude specific apps, domains, or entire website categories from monitoring. Pricing: Free tier available, with a Team plan starting around $17/month for organization-wide rollout (individual Plus-tier pricing not clearly published in source data).
✅ Benefits
  • Genuinely eliminates the "recap your day" problem for people juggling many small context switches — since it already has the context from screen activity, it can answer "what did I do today" without manual logging
  • Privacy design is more granular than many competitors — it reads structured text rather than storing screenshots, and lets users exclude specific apps, domains, or entire categories of sites from being monitored
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Requires trusting an AI assistant with continuous access to on-screen activity across all applications, which is a meaningfully higher privacy/security bar than tools that only see explicitly shared context (like a single meeting recording)
  • As a very new product (launched March 2026), it currently lacks team-collaboration features and has a much smaller track record than established meeting/notetaking competitors like Granola or Otter
