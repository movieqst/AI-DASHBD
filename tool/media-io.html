Overview
Wondershare Media.io is an all-in-one AI creative platform that combines AI image generation, AI video generation, AI audio tools, and professional editing tools in a single platform. In 2026, the platform announced a major update featuring unlimited free AI image generation powered by the ToMoviee Lite model, removing the credit-based limitations common among competitors. New dedicated workflows include AI Story (turning long-form text of up to 5,000 characters into AI-generated videos with character consistency across scenes) and AI Ads (generating campaign-ready video ads from product images or URLs).
