Overview
Nebius AI Cloud is an AI-centric cloud platform providing high-performance infrastructure for machine learning training and inference workloads . The platform offers a comprehensive suite of services for AI development including virtual machines with NVIDIA GPU support, GPU clusters connected via InfiniBand networks for high-speed distributed computing, Slurm workload manager clusters for ML/AI experiments, and Kubernetes clusters with GPU and InfiniBand support . Beyond compute, Nebius provides object storage (AWS S3-compatible) for ML/AI datasets and model artifacts, managed PostgreSQL database clusters, and a container registry for Docker image storage . The platform also offers specialized AI services including Serverless AI (containerized inference endpoints and background jobs), managed MLflow clusters for experiment tracking and model registry, and turnkey applications like JupyterLab, vLLM, and Open WebUI . Third-party integrations support running and orchestrating AI workloads with tools like SkyPilot and Run:ai . Nebius is designed for developers, data scientists, and ML engineers who need scalable, high-performance infrastructure for training and deploying AI models . The platform includes observability tools (metrics and alerts, logs, traces), identity and access management, comprehensive documentation, and support for Terraform and CLI tools . The platform is GDPR-compliant with features for data portability and deletion . Pricing follows a cloud consumption model with transparent costs for compute, storage, and networking resources .
✅ Benefits
  • Comprehensive AI infrastructure platform with GPU compute, high-speed InfiniBand networking, Slurm and Kubernetes clusters, and managed MLflow .
  • Serverless AI endpoints and background jobs for containerized inference and training, plus turnkey applications like JupyterLab and vLLM .
  • S3-compatible object storage and third-party integrations (SkyPilot, Run:ai) make it easy to migrate existing AI workloads .
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • As a specialized AI cloud, Nebius may not offer the breadth of general-purpose cloud services available on AWS, Azure, or GCP .
  • Pricing is consumption-based; teams should model expected GPU usage before committing to a deployment .
  • The platform's focus on AI/ML means it's not suitable for traditional web applications or non-AI workloads .
