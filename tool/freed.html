Overview
Freed is an AI-powered clinical documentation assistant designed for physicians, nurse practitioners, and other clinicians. Like Abridge, Freed listens to patient-clinician conversations and automatically generates complete SOAP notes (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan). However, Freed emphasizes extreme simplicity: clinicians just tap a 'Capture' button, and the AI scribe creates the entire note instantly—no complex setup or configuration required. The platform works out of the box without training, but can be trained to learn each clinician's individual style and adapt to their preferences. Freed is available across any device—phone, tablet, or desktop—and integrates with popular EHRs including Athena and eClinicalWorks on desktop. The platform is fully HIPAA, HITECH, and SOC2 compliant. The app is available on both iOS and Android. Freed operates on a subscription model with a monthly subscription costing approximately $179. A free trial is available for clinicians to test the platform before committing. The company does not collect any user data from the app. Freed is positioned as the tool for clinicians who want a simpler, faster alternative to traditional dictation services, with a focus on allowing doctors to 'leave work at work' and reclaim their weekends.
✅ Benefits
  • Extremely simple workflow: one 'Capture' button generates a complete SOAP note instantly—no complex setup, training, or workflow changes required
  • Available on both iOS and Android (unlike competitor Abridge), plus desktop, making it accessible across the full range of clinician devices
  • Can be trained to learn each clinician's individual style and preferences, improving accuracy and personalization over time
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Pricing is steep at approximately $179/month, which may be prohibitive for individual clinicians or small practices
  • As an ambient AI scribe, it requires a clear audio environment; noisy clinics or overlapping speech may impact accuracy
  • Android app may not be available in all regions; users should confirm availability in their country's Google Play Store
