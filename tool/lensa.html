Overview
Lensa is a mobile-first AI photo editing app from Prisma AI, available on iOS and Android. As of mid-2026, the app runs version 8.8.3 and offers AI-powered photo retouching, Magic Avatars (AI-generated stylized avatars from user selfies), background removal, and one-tap enhancement tools. Lensa gained significant popularity in late 2022 with its Magic Avatars feature and has maintained a strong user base. The app operates on a freemium subscription model with in-app purchases: Unlimited Access Weekly ($2.99), Monthly ($4.99), Annual ($29.99-$49.99 depending on tier), and one-time Magic Avatar packs ranging from $3.99 to $11.99. The app supports 9 languages and is available in the US, Russia, and Brazil, with approximately 50,000 monthly downloads and $300,000 in monthly revenue. Content rating is 4+.
✅ Benefits
  • Best-in-class mobile AI photo editing experience with extensive retouching tools
  • Magic Avatars feature remains a popular differentiator for generating stylized profile pictures
  • Broad language support (9 languages including English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish)
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Subscription-first with no clear free tier beyond limited features—annual pricing ranges from $29.99-$49.99
  • Limited desktop or web presence; mobile-only experience
  • Advanced features (Magic Avatars packs) are locked behind one-time in-app purchases on top of subscription
