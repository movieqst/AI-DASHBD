Overview
Higgsfield AI is a multi-model video generation platform founded in 2023 by ex-Google Brain engineers, now valued at roughly $1.3 billion following a January 2026 Series A extension. Unlike most competitors that lock users into a single model, Higgsfield aggregates multiple leading video generators — including Sora 2, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, and Seedance 2.0 — under one subscription, letting creators compare outputs and choose the best model for each project. The platform is positioned as an all-in-one creative workspace for AI image and video production, with creator presets, fast visual testing, and short-form concept development workflows. Pricing as of mid-2026 runs on a credit-based system across three tiers: Starter ($15/month, 200 credits), Plus ($39/month, 1,000 credits), and Ultra ($99/month, 3,000 credits, scalable to 9,000). A free tier offers 10 daily credits for exploration. Credit consumption varies dramatically by model — Kling 3.0 costs roughly 6 credits per video, while Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 run 40–70 credits each. Credits expire after 90 days and monthly plan credits do not roll over, meaning users must budget carefully. The platform also offers a "365-Day Unlimited" perk, though this applies primarily to image generation rather than video. User feedback highlights the platform's strength in broad model exploration and fast iteration, making it ideal for filmmakers testing visual concepts before committing to a production pipeline. However, reviewers note it lacks deeper production features like shot tracking, reusable references, dailies review, role-based collaboration, and editor handoff — meaning serious film productions still need additional workflow tools beyond Higgsfield's generation layer. The credit-based pricing can also become unpredictable for heavy users who iterate frequently. Stacked against similar all-in-one platforms, Higgsfield sits in the middle on price and squarely in the generation-layer camp rather than the finished-video camp. Its $15/$39/$99 tiers land above OpenArt's $7/$14.50 entry points but well under InVideo AI's $100-120/month Generative tier, which is the only one of the three that also writes scripts, adds voiceover, and edits captions around the generated footage rather than handing back a raw clip.
✅ Benefits
  • Aggregates multiple top-tier video models (Sora 2, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.0) in one subscription, eliminating the need for separate accounts across platforms
  • Fast visual testing and creator presets make it excellent for concept development and pre-visualization before entering a full production pipeline
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Credit-based pricing is unpredictable at scale — high-end models like Sora 2 can consume 40–70 credits per video, and credits expire after 90 days without rollover
  • Lacks production-grade workflow features like shot tracking, dailies review, and role-based collaboration, so it cannot serve as a complete filmmaking pipeline on its own
