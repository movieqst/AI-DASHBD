Overview
Grammarly is a long-standing AI writing assistant that checks grammar, spelling, punctuation, tone, and clarity, and increasingly generates and rewrites text outright via its GrammarlyGO/generative features. It works as a browser extension, desktop app, and mobile keyboard, and integrates with Google Docs, MS Office, and most web forms. Pricing in 2026 is three-tiered: Free ($0, basic grammar/spelling/tone checks with roughly 100 AI prompts/month), Premium/Pro (around $12/month billed annually, or up to $30/month billed monthly — jumps to 2,000 AI prompts, full-sentence rewrites, plagiarism detection, and vocabulary/clarity suggestions), and Business (~$15–25/member/month, adding brand tone controls, team analytics, and SSO). Following Grammarly's 2025 acquisition of Superhuman (email) and Coda (workspace), the parent company rebranded itself "Superhuman" in October 2025 and now bundles all three products, plus a new proactive assistant called Superhuman Go, into a broader suite.
✅ Benefits
  • Deep coverage across virtually every writing surface (browser, desktop, Office, Google Docs, mobile keyboard) makes it convenient for real-time editing wherever text is written, without switching apps
  • The free tier is genuinely useful on its own, covering core grammar/spelling/tone checks, so casual users get real value without paying
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Billed-monthly pricing is dramatically higher than annual ($30/mo monthly vs. ~$12/mo annual), a gap many users miss until they see the bill, and there's no standalone trial of the paid tier
  • The company faced public criticism in March 2026 after its "Expert Review" feature briefly attached real writers' names to AI-generated feedback without consent — worth knowing when evaluating trust in its AI-authored suggestions
