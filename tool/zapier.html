Overview
Zapier is the largest no-code workflow automation platform, connecting over 7,000–9,000 apps through simple trigger-and-action "Zaps." In 2026 its major addition is Zapier Central/AI agents — persistent AI assistants that can monitor data, reason through logic using models like GPT-5 and Claude, and execute multi-step tasks autonomously across connected apps rather than following rigid pre-set steps. Pricing is task-based across four tiers: Free (100 tasks/month, 2-step Zaps only), Professional (from $29.99/month, 750 tasks, unlimited multi-step Zaps), Team (from ~$103.50/month, 2,000+ tasks, shared workspaces, up to 25 users), and Enterprise (custom, often tens of thousands of dollars/year for mid-market deployments). AI agent usage is billed separately/incrementally on top of the task allowance.
✅ Benefits
  • Unmatched breadth of integrations (7,000+ apps) means it can almost always connect to whatever niche or mainstream tool a business already uses, with the easiest, most beginner-friendly interface among automation platforms
  • New AI agent capabilities (Zapier Central) let workflows make decisions and act autonomously rather than following only rigid trigger-action logic
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Pricing has risen substantially and is widely reported as the most expensive option per task compared to alternatives like Make.com or n8n (often 3–5x more at scale)
  • The free tier was cut from 750 tasks to just 100 tasks in recent years, making it usable only for light testing rather than any real production workflow
