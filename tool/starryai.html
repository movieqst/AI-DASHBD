Overview
StarryAI is a mobile AI art generation app supporting iOS and Android platforms, designed for users who want to create stunning visuals from text prompts or images. The platform offers a free tier with 5 generations per day, all of which are completely watermark-free and owned by the creator—a notable differentiator from many free-tier competitors. Users can choose from a variety of artistic styles and models, adjust aspect ratios, and set initial images as starting points. StarryAI's core features include batch generation (creating multiple works at once), image evolution (iterative refinement of outputs), image upscaling, and smart fill for completing missing parts of images. The platform also supports image variation generation and AI portrait creation, with a community feed where users can view and be inspired by others' creations. For creators interested in the NFT space, StarryAI provides integrated NFT publishing capabilities, allowing users to tokenize their AI-generated artwork.
✅ Benefits
  • Daily free generations (5 per day) are completely watermark-free and owned by the creator—a genuine differentiator from many free-tier competitors
  • Batch generation, image evolution, and smart fill provide more creative control than basic text-to-image generators
  • Integrated NFT publishing capability appeals to creators interested in the Web3 art space
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Free tier's daily allowance is limited compared to unlimited generation platforms like Dream by Wombo
  • Some advanced styles require 2 'coins' per generation, with only 1 free coin awarded daily, creating a paywall for certain aesthetic options
  • Relies heavily on mobile app interface, which may feel less versatile than web-based platforms for some users
