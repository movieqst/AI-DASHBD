Overview
OpenArt is a credit-based AI creative platform that bundles image generation, video creation, upscaling, editing, and character consistency tools under one subscription, positioning itself as a replacement for juggling multiple specialist tools. As of mid-2026, the platform offers access to 100+ AI models including FLUX, Veo 3, GPT Image, Kling 2.1, and Nano Banana, plus unique features like one-click story creation, consistent character training (up to 80+ characters on higher tiers), and an image editing suite with inpainting, outpainting, and ControlNet. Founded in 2022 by former Google engineers, the platform targets digital artists, marketers, and small agencies who need breadth across media types rather than depth in a single category. Pricing is credit-based across five tiers: Free ($0, 40–50 one-time trial credits plus limited daily basic-model generations, no commercial rights, no video), Essential ($14/month or $7/month annually, ~4,000 credits, ~4,000 images or ~50 videos, no commercial rights), Advanced ($29/month or $14.50/month annually, ~12,000 credits, commercial rights unlocked, ~150 videos), Infinite ($56/month or $28/month annually, ~24,000 credits, unlimited generation on select models), and Wonder ($240/month or $120/month annually, ~106,000 credits, 32 parallel generations). Credit costs vary by action: a standard 1024×1024 image costs 1 credit, while a 5-second Kling 2.1 video costs 500 credits and Veo 3 normal video costs 1,500 credits. Base subscription credits expire monthly and do not roll over; add-on credits ($15 for ~5,000) do roll over. User feedback is sharply divided. Positive reviewers praise the consolidation value — replacing separate subscriptions for image, video, and editing tools with one platform at a competitive entry price. The consistent character tools are particularly valued by visual novel creators and marketers maintaining brand assets across campaigns. However, Trustpilot scores hover around 3.5/5, with the most common complaints centering on credit loss from failed generations, strict refund policies, post-cancellation billing disputes, and customer support response times ranging from days to over a week. The free tier is extremely limited — 40 one-time credits are exhausted in minutes — and commercial rights are gated behind the $14.50/month Advanced plan, making the cheaper Essential tier unsuitable for client work. Against comparable multi-model platforms, OpenArt is the cheapest way in — its $7/month Essential tier undercuts both Higgsfield's $15/month Starter and InVideo AI's $20/month Plus — but that low price reflects its focus on image generation with video as a secondary feature, rather than a video-first workflow. Higgsfield and InVideo AI both put more emphasis on video model breadth and production-style features, so OpenArt is the stronger pick for creators whose main output is images and who only need video occasionally.
✅ Benefits
  • Consolidates 100+ AI models for image generation, video, upscaling, and editing into one subscription — undercutting the cost of stacking separate specialist tools like Midjourney, Runway, and Topaz
  • Strong consistent character and one-click story tools enable visual novel creators and marketing teams to maintain style continuity across dozens of scenes without manual redrawing
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Base subscription credits expire monthly with no rollover, and failed generations frequently still consume credits — a combination that wastes budget and frustrates heavy experimenters
  • Commercial usage rights are locked behind the $14.50/month Advanced plan, rendering the $7/month Essential tier unsuitable for any paid client work
