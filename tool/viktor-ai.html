Overview
Viktor is an AI "coworker" that lives natively inside Slack and Microsoft Teams rather than a separate chat window. Instead of only answering questions, it runs in its own cloud compute environment where it can write and execute code, connect to 3,000+ external tools (Salesforce, HubSpot, Linear, Notion, Jira, Stripe, GitHub, Google Drive, and more), and carry out autonomous workflows lasting up to several weeks — tracking deadlines, updating stakeholders, and maintaining context without repeated prompting. It builds persistent workspace memory over time, effectively learning company-specific "skills" it can reuse, and proactively monitors metrics/data to suggest or take action rather than waiting to be asked.
✅ Benefits
  • Native Slack/Teams presence keeps AI-driven work inside a team's existing communication channels and threads, avoiding the context-switching cost of a separate chatbot tab
  • Long-running autonomous workflows (spanning weeks) and persistent memory of how a specific company operates make it closer to a junior teammate than a typical single-session chatbot
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • As a newer, less widely reviewed product, independent benchmarking, pricing transparency, and long-term reliability data are far more limited than for established competitors
  • Giving an AI agent its own cloud compute environment with code execution and access to thousands of connected business tools raises meaningfully higher security/governance considerations than a standard read-only chatbot
