Overview
Fotor is an all-in-one AI photo editing toolbox that positions itself as the editor where 'editing used to be a task, now it's just a talk'. The platform's flagship feature is the AI Agent, which allows users to upload a photo and simply say or type what they want—the AI understands natural language commands and applies multiple edits simultaneously, eliminating the need for complex manual adjustments. This makes professional-quality photo editing accessible to creators, photo enthusiasts, and casual users alike. The platform serves as a comprehensive toolkit with a broad range of AI-powered features: AI Photo Enhancer for sharpening blurry, grainy, or low-resolution images; Magic Eraser for removing unwanted elements from photos and videos; Background Remover for one-click subject extraction; AI Retouch for skin smoothing and blemish removal; AI Headshot Generator for professional-quality avatars; Text-to-Image and Text-to-Video generation; and creative effects like 3D Cartoon and AI art transformations. Fotor also includes traditional photo editing tools for brightness, contrast, saturation, curves, hue, and grain adjustment, as well as unique AI features like AI Hairstyle, AI Expand for extending photo subjects and backgrounds, and photo restoration for old family photos. The platform is available across mobile and web, with a free tier offering basic functionality and Fotor Pro subscriptions available on weekly, monthly, or yearly plans. Fotor is positioned as a versatile, one-stop solution for users who need quick, AI-driven photo enhancements without the learning curve of traditional editing software.
