Overview
CapCut is ByteDance's all-in-one video editing platform, deeply integrated with TikTok's ecosystem and increasingly powered by AI features for short-form content creation. As of mid-2026, the AI layer includes auto-captions with animated text and emoji highlights, AI background removal, smart cutout, auto-reframe for multi-platform export, batch processing for up to 20 clips simultaneously, and a 2026-added Director Mode for short dramas. The platform runs across mobile (iOS/Android), desktop (Windows/Mac with GPU acceleration), and web, with the mobile app offering roughly 90% of desktop functionality. It has become the default editor for TikTok creators, with 68% of video production courses now including CapCut in their curriculum. Pricing follows a freemium model: Free ($0, all core AI features included but watermarked exports, no 4K export, no cloud storage for new web spaces), Pro ($19.99/month or $179.99/year — ~$15/month effective — watermark removal, 4K export, premium templates, 1,200 AI points, team collaboration), and Team (variable pricing, typically $15–$30/seat/month annually, shared assets, centralized billing). AI credit top-ups start at $4.99 for 100 credits. The platform requires no specialized hardware and leverages GPU acceleration where available. User feedback presents a stark split: the Apple App Store rating is 4.6/5 from 1.1 million reviews, while Trustpilot sits at 1.3/5 from over 1,000 reviews (86% one-star). App Store users praise the editing speed, template library, and intuitive mobile interface. Trustpilot complaints cluster on billing — forced price hikes at renewal (old $10/month plans cancelled and replaced with $20/month), continued charges after cancellation, account lockouts near renewal, and email-only support with 2–4 week response times. The free tier's recent restriction to 6 exports per week before watermarking has also drawn criticism. For high-volume short-form creators, CapCut Pro delivers real time savings; for casual users, the free tier's new limitations make it increasingly difficult to rely on without paying.
