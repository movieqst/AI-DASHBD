Overview
Nscale is a vertically integrated AI cloud provider headquartered in Europe, purpose-built to deliver enterprise-grade AI infrastructure at massive scale . The company offers a comprehensive suite of AI solutions including owned and colocated data centres, GPU clusters, orchestration, AI services, managed software, and private cloud enhancements . Its architecture spans GW+ greenfield data centres across Europe and North America, enabling customers to run efficient and scalable AI training, fine-tuning, and inferencing workloads . Nscale has emerged as a major player in the global AI infrastructure market, signing landmark agreements with Microsoft to deploy approximately 200,000 NVIDIA GB300 GPUs across Europe and the U.S. . This includes a ~240MW hyperscale AI campus in Texas delivering 104,000 GPUs from Q3 2026, plus deployments in Portugal, the UK, and Norway . The company also announced Stargate UK with OpenAI and NVIDIA, and Stargate Norway with Aker ASA, positioning itself as a key sovereign AI infrastructure provider for Europe . Nscale raised the largest Series B in UK and European history at $1.1 billion in September 2025, led by Aker ASA with participation from NVIDIA, Dell, Fidelity, Blue Owl, and others . The company's vertically integrated model and access to low-cost renewable energy enable it to deliver state-of-the-art AI infrastructure more quickly and at lower cost than industry benchmarks. With a leadership team that has built over 50 data centers and scaled multi-billion-dollar infrastructure businesses, Nscale is setting a new standard for how the next wave of AI infrastructure is delivered globally .
✅ Benefits
  • Vertically integrated AI infrastructure platform covering everything from greenfield data centers and GPU clusters to managed software and AI services.
  • Signed massive contracts with Microsoft for ~200,000 NVIDIA GB300 GPUs across Europe and the U.S., demonstrating world-class execution capability.
  • $1.1 billion Series B (largest in European history) with backing from NVIDIA, Dell, Fidelity, and Aker provides strong financial runway.
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Pricing is not publicly disclosed; requires contacting Nscale for custom enterprise quotes.
  • As a relatively young company (founded ~2020), Nscale has a shorter track record than established cloud providers like AWS or Azure.
  • The company's focus on hyperscale infrastructure is built for enterprise customers—not suitable for individual developers or small teams.
