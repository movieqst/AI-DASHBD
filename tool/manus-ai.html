Overview
Manus is a general-purpose autonomous AI agent that operates inside a "virtual computer" — it can browse the web in real time, write and execute code, analyze data, build websites, generate slides, and deliver finished results from a single prompt, rather than just answering questions. It runs on the Manus 1.6 model family (Lite, standard, and Max) and added Scheduled Tasks 2.0 plus native connectors (Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, GitHub, Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram) through 2026. Meta had agreed to acquire Manus for roughly $2 billion in December 2025, but Chinese regulators blocked the deal in April 2026, and Manus remains independent. Pricing is credit-based rather than flat-rate: Free ($0, 300 daily-refresh credits, 1 concurrent task), Pro tiers at $20/month (4,000 monthly credits) and $40/month (8,000 credits), a top "Extended" tier at $200/month (40,000 credits), and Team plans from $20–40/seat/month.
✅ Benefits
  • True end-to-end task execution (not just chat) — it can independently plan, browse, code, and deliver a finished deliverable (document, website, itinerary) from one instruction
  • The free tier is described as genuinely usable rather than a stripped-down trial, giving real daily credits rather than a one-time allotment
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Credit-based pricing makes costs unpredictable at scale — complex or long-running tasks can consume credits quickly, and unused monthly credits don't roll over
  • Reliability is capped by the underlying "LLM-planner" limitation shared by all autonomous agents: it has more tools than a chatbot but not fundamentally more reliable planning, so failure modes like broken clicks or stalled browser tasks still occur
