Overview
PhotoRoom is an AI-powered photo editing platform purpose-built for e-commerce sellers, resellers, and small brands who need fast, consistent product photography at scale. As of mid-2026, the platform's core toolkit includes instant background removal, AI-generated backgrounds and scenes, Virtual Model (dressing AI-generated models in apparel), Ghost Mannequin, Product Staging, batch processing, and direct Shopify listing creation. The mobile app is widely regarded as best-in-class for on-the-go sellers, while the web version supports higher-volume workflows. The platform processes millions of images monthly for Poshmark, Depop, eBay, and Shopify sellers. Pricing runs across five tiers: Free ($0, 250 white-background exports/month with PhotoRoom watermark, limited AI features, no commercial use), Pro ($7.99/month or $7.50/month billed annually — $90/year, unlimited manual exports, advanced AI tools, 500 batch exports/month, 5× Free AI credit allowance, high-resolution export, up to 50 seats), Max ($26.99/month or $20.99/month annually, better AI models, 1,500 batch exports, 3× Pro AI credits, Shopify publishing, priority support), Ultra ($99–$990/month depending on sub-tier x1 to x10, 5,000–50,000 batch exports, best AI models, video generator), and Enterprise (custom, API access, SOC 2 Type 2, 200,000+ images/year minimum). API pricing is separate: Basic at $20/month ($0.02/image) for background removal, Plus at $100/month ($0.10/image) for full image editing. User feedback is strongest among mobile-first resellers who praise the speed and quality of background removal and the Virtual Model feature for eliminating photoshoot costs. Independent reviews rate PhotoRoom 8.9/10 for e-commerce photography specifically. However, common complaints include confusing dual-meter billing (AI credits and batch export limits are tracked separately, leading to unexpected overages), frequent pricing changes that surprise existing users, and a web interface that feels less polished than the mobile app. The free tier's watermark makes it unusable for any commercial listing, and the jump from Pro ($7.50/mo) to Max ($20.99/mo) is steep for growing sellers who need Shopify integration. For full-time resellers, PhotoRoom is a clear ROI winner; for occasional sellers, the free tier's limitations push toward paid plans quickly.
✅ Benefits
  • Best-in-class mobile app for e-commerce photography — background removal, AI-generated scenes, and Virtual Model features eliminate traditional photoshoot costs for resellers and small brands
  • Pro plan at $7.50/month annually is among the cheapest AI photo editors on the market, with batch processing up to 500 images/month turning hours of manual work into minutes
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Dual-meter billing (separate AI credits and batch export limits) creates genuine confusion and unexpected overages when one meter runs out before the other
  • Pricing structure changes frequently without clear notice, with users reporting surprise rate hikes and weekly billing options that feel designed to obscure true monthly costs
