Overview
Range is a team collaboration and async-check-in platform (not a general-purpose AI chatbot) designed to help distributed and remote teams stay aligned without constant meetings. Its three core integrated tools are: daily/async check-ins (so team members know what colleagues are working on without a live status meeting), Goals/OKR tracking that connects to daily workflows to build alignment and transparency at scale, and recurring-meeting agenda tools that let managers share notes with attendees automatically after each meeting and assign action items that surface in team members' task lists. It integrates with GitHub, Jira, Google Calendar, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Trello, GitLab, Bitbucket, and other common workplace tools, and can be accessed directly from Slack for check-ins. Pricing starts around $8/user/month, with a free tier/trial (no credit card required) available.
✅ Benefits
  • The daily check-in format genuinely helps build team culture and cross-team visibility for distributed/remote teams, with users specifically citing improved cohesion and knowing colleagues "as people" rather than just task-completers
  • Deep integration with existing developer and workplace tools (GitHub, Jira, Slack, Google Calendar) lets check-ins and goal-tracking surface directly inside tools teams already use daily, rather than requiring a separate destination app
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • Some users report the tool works best only in a healthy team culture — in environments with interpersonal tension or micromanaging, the same daily-check-in format can reportedly become a tool for surveillance rather than connection
  • Users note occasional friction: some third-party integrations (GitHub, Jira, Google Calendar) give "questionable suggestions," and parts of the interface (like the Objectives/OKR section) are reported as difficult to navigate with hidden functionality that takes time to discover
