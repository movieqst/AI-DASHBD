Overview
Writesonic began as an AI writing tool (blog articles, ad copy, product descriptions, and 80+ other content templates using models like GPT-4o and Claude) but has repositioned itself in 2026 as an "AI Search Visibility Platform," combining traditional SEO tooling with Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — tracking how a brand appears (or fails to appear) in AI-generated answers across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, then using AI agents to suggest or make fixes. 2026 pricing runs from a permanent Free plan (10,000 words/month, access to GPT-4o mini and Claude Haiku with usage limits) through paid tiers — Lite/Starter (~$39–49/month annual), Standard (~$79/month annual, the most popular tier — 1,000 credits, full model access, SEO tools), Professional (~$249/month, where GEO/AI-visibility tracking unlocks), and Growth/Enterprise (up to $399/month or custom).
✅ Benefits
  • Bundles AI writing with SEO tooling and (at higher tiers) AI-search-visibility tracking in one subscription, which can consolidate what would otherwise require several separate tools for content-and-visibility-focused marketing teams
  • The permanent free tier (10,000 words/month, access to real models) is genuinely usable for testing the platform, more generous than many rivals' time-limited trials
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • The most differentiated features — GEO/AI-visibility tracking that sets it apart from a plain AI writer — are locked behind the $249+/month Professional tier, out of reach for many individual users or small teams
  • For pure writing quality, some users report output comparable to general chatbots like ChatGPT at a meaningfully higher price, meaning the value proposition rests specifically on the bundled SEO/GEO tooling rather than raw writing capability
