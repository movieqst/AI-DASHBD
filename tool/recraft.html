Overview
Recraft is a uniquely positioned AI image generator that distinguishes itself by producing genuinely editable vector graphics (SVG files) from text prompts, making it the only tool in its class for professional design workflows. While most AI image generators output pixel-based raster images, Recraft's architecture returns production-ready SVGs with smooth paths and organized layers, eliminating the need for tracing or cleanup—ideal for logos, icons, illustrations, and brand assets. The platform's model family has evolved through several generations, with Recraft V2 (released March 2024) being the first model trained from scratch by the company, featuring 20 billion parameters and breakthrough human anatomical accuracy. V2 introduced vector image generation and brand consistency inputs, and remains popular for its curated styles like Plastic 3D, Linocut, Grain, and Vector Art. The newer Recraft V4 series includes specialized variants: V4 for fast concepting, V4 Pro for super-resolution realism, V4 Vector for clean scalable graphics, and V4 Pro Vector for production-grade illustrations. Recraft operates as a comprehensive workspace rather than a single-output generator, allowing users to generate, edit, and organize assets with advanced style control. The platform supports brand consistency through custom style training and includes features like background removal, vectorization, inpainting, and upscaling. All V4 models are available on every plan including the Free tier, with paid plans unlocking commercial rights and API access. The platform is particularly valued by designers, branding professionals, and marketing teams who need consistent, high-quality, and commercially usable vector and raster visuals. It also integrates with third-party models like Nano Banana 2 and GPT Image 2 within the same workspace.
✅ Benefits
  • The only AI image generator that produces truly editable vector files (SVG) with smooth paths and organized layers—production-ready without tracing or cleanup
  • Provides specialized V4 model variants (V4, V4 Pro, V4 Vector, V4 Pro Vector) optimized for different use cases from concepting to production-grade illustrations
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • V2 models, while still available, lack accurate in-image text generation and may produce typographic errors compared to V3/V4 models
  • Some style names exist across different model versions but produce visually different results, requiring users to test which model fits their aesthetic needs
