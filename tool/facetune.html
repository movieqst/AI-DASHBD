Overview
Facetune is a specialized AI-powered photo and video editing app focused entirely on portrait and selfie enhancement, serving social media creators, influencers, and everyday users who want polished, natural-looking selfies. The platform's flagship feature is its AI Assistant, which allows users to edit photos through natural language commands—simply type or say what you want (e.g., 'whiten my teeth,' 'remove my eye bags,' 'smooth my skin') and the AI applies the changes automatically. This conversational interface eliminates the need to navigate complex editing menus, making professional-quality retouching accessible to users with no prior experience. Beyond its AI Assistant, Facetune offers a comprehensive suite of portrait editing tools: virtual makeup application with beauty looks presets, hairstyle and hair color swapping, photo retouch (blemish removal, teeth whitening, under-eye concealment), background removal and replacement, object removal, and image enhancement. The app also includes video retouching capabilities, allowing consistent enhancements across video content. Facetune is available on iOS and Android with a touch-friendly mobile interface optimized for on-the-go editing, and free tier provides basic functionality with advanced features locked behind subscription. The platform is positioned as a style playground where users can experiment with different looks and maintain a vibrant, varied social media presence.
✅ Benefits
  • AI Assistant enables natural language photo editing—users describe what they want in plain English and the AI applies the changes instantly
  • Specializes in natural-looking facial retouching with professional-grade tools for blemish removal, teeth whitening, skin smoothing, and virtual makeup
⚠️ Drawbacks
