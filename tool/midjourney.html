Overview
Midjourney is the leading closed-source AI image generation platform, operating through Discord and a redesigned web interface at alpha.midjourney.com. As of mid-2026, the platform launched V8 Alpha on March 17, 2026—a complete rewrite delivering 5x faster generation, native 2K HD images via the --hd parameter, dramatically improved text rendering (readable street signs, product labels, typography), and superior prompt understanding. V8 introduces new creative parameters including --chaos, --weird, --exp, and --raw for experimental control. Premium features cost 4x standard generation: --hd, --q 4 (enhanced coherence), style references, and moodboards. Pricing is subscription-based with GPU-hour allocations: Basic ($10/month, ~3.3 fast hours, no Relax Mode), Standard ($30/month, 15 fast hours + unlimited Relax), Pro ($60/month, 30 hours + Stealth Mode), Mega ($120/month, 60 hours + Stealth). Annual billing offers 20% savings. Relax Mode provides unlimited but slower generations after fast hours are depleted (Standard and above). Extra GPU hours cost ~$4/hour. There is no public API—Midjourney remains a closed ecosystem.
✅ Benefits
  • Best-in-class aesthetic quality for artistic, painterly, and cinematic outputs—consistently rated ahead of competitors for creative work
  • V8's 5x speed improvement and native 2K resolution (--hd) dramatically improve workflow compared to V7
  • Relax Mode prevents hard cutoffs; users with Standard+ plans can continue generating (slower) after fast hours run out
⚠️ Drawbacks
  • No API or programmatic access—all interaction is through Discord or the web interface, making automation impossible
  • No free tier; the Basic plan's 3.3 fast hours with no Relax Mode functions more as a trial than a sustainable option
  • GPU-hour pricing is opaque and hard to predict—users cannot easily estimate how many images their hours will produce
